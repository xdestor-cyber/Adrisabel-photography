# Creative treatment — "Once Upon a Tiny Time"

## The idea

Every baby is a story, and Adrisabel's sessions are its chapters.

The video is a storybook that opens in a mother's hands. It starts the way every beloved story starts, *"Once upon a tiny time…"*, and the first ten seconds are pure feeling: ten little toes, one sleepy yawn, a love that changed everything. A gold heart becomes the doorway to a rosewood-and-gold book cover: **Adrisabel**.

The book opens to a contents page, *"Every baby is a story. Which chapter will you treasure forever?"*, and each package becomes a chapter that follows the first year of a baby's life:

| Chapter | Package | Emotional reason to exist |
|---|---|---|
| One | **Sunshine** | *Hello, little sunshine.* Those first sleepy newborn days, all about baby. |
| Two | **Wonderland** | *Welcome to the family.* Everyone who loves this baby, in one frame. |
| Three | **Fairytale** | *Look who's smiling now!* The months when personality blooms. |
| Four | **Pixie Dust** | *Every little milestone of their first year…* The magic of watching them grow. |
| Five | **Cake Smash** | *Then comes the big ONE!* The first-birthday celebration. |
| Six | **Seaside Beach** | *Sandy toes & salty kisses.* The whole family, barefoot at South Padre Island. |

The story closes as the book does: *"Your once upon a time starts here."* The back cover carries the invitation, **Visit adrisabel.com**.

A mother can recognize her own chapter: "we're about to have our newborn", "my baby just started smiling", "I want her whole first year". The chapter order doubles as the order in which those moments arrive.

## Why this works as an advertisement

- **Desire before detail.** Each chapter opens on an emotional headline and a hand-drawn moment. Only then does the illustration step aside for name → price → who it's for → what's included. The viewer thinks "I love this" first and "I understand what I'm booking" second.
- **One story, not a price list.** Page turns, a nightfall, a confetti burst, and a rolling wave connect the chapters, so the offers feel like one journey through the first year.
- **Pixie Dust as the centerpiece.** It gets the most time (25 s), the key change in the music, and its own visual metaphor: waxing moons, one per milestone session. Moon phases make "5 sessions across the first year" instantly understandable, and the two schedules become two rows of moons with their own "$1,000 total" price, joined by a clear "or".
- **Website first.** adrisabel.com appears as a quiet running footer on the chapter pages and on the booking page. On the final card it is the dominant, largest element. Instagram and phone sit beneath it in decreasing emphasis.
- **Warm, never pushy.** No countdowns, discounts, or "limited spots". The desire comes from love and imagination.

## Visual system

- **Style:** gold-ink line illustrations that draw themselves, over soft watercolor washes, on warm cream paper, like a luxury picture book. Pastel fills are printed slightly off-register for a hand-made, printed feel. Everything is original: hand-authored SVG illustrations and procedurally generated watercolor, paper, and linen textures. There are **no photographs** (stock, AI, or otherwise), so nothing could be mistaken for Adrisabel's client work.
- **Palette:** cream `#FBF5EE`, ink plum `#3B2630`, gold `#B8883C` / `#EBCB8B`, rosewood `#7A4452` (brand cover, prices, badges), midnight `#1E2250` (Pixie Dust), plus one watercolor per chapter: butter, lavender, sky, strawberry, aqua and sand.
- **Type:** *Great Vibes* script for the wordmark and the "once upon a time" voice · *Cormorant Garamond* for package names, headlines and prices (lining numerals for clarity) · *Jost* for inclusions, details and contact information. All three are SIL Open Font License.
- **Layout:** one consistent chapter template so viewers learn where to look: kicker → headline → illustration → **NAME** → **price** → type → highlighted badge (baby only / family / ages / cake / location) → icon stats. All essential text sits between 240 and 1480 px vertically, clear of Reels/TikTok/Shorts interface overlays.

## Motion language

- Real 3D page turns around the book's spine, with page shading and a paper back face.
- Special transitions: a heart-shaped iris into the cover, a sparkling diagonal *nightfall* into Pixie Dust, a confetti iris into Cake Smash, and a rolling wave into Seaside Beach.
- Deterministic particles: gold dust, sparkle bursts on price reveals, confetti, a wand trail, and a twinkling star field.
- Ambient life on every page: sun rays turn, hearts float, waves roll, the candle flickers, washes breathe.
- Every cut and reveal sits on the musical grid (104 BPM). Prices land on beats, inclusions pop on successive beats, and the website lands on the final resolving downbeat.

## Music & sound

An **original composition** written for this video (`music/compose.py`), rendered with the MIT-licensed FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont and mixed in `music/mix.py`:

- Piano, celesta, glockenspiel, strings, harp, flute, pizzicato, nylon guitar, bass and light percussion. It's warm and joyful, not a lullaby and not a jingle, with no vocals.
- **Form follows the story:** an intimate piano-and-celesta intro; strings bloom under the logo; a groove starts at Chapter One; whimsical pizzicato for Fairytale; a whole-step **key change** as night falls on Pixie Dust; a bouncy Cake Smash theme; a breezy flute for the beach; then a IV–V–I resolution that lands exactly as **adrisabel.com** appears. A celesta echo of the opening motif closes it.
- **Sound design** is synthesized and placed from the animation's own cue list: paper page-turns, sparkle bells tuned to the song's key, soft pops for inclusions, a party popper, and an ocean swell. It is mixed 7–18 dB under the music.
- Mastered to −14.5 LUFS integrated / −1 dBTP for social platforms.
